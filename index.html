About

Where data meets design

I’m a data science and public policy student at the University of Chicago. Most of my work sits where policy questions meet messy real-world data — conflict records, sentencing files, census tracts, healthcare payments. The through-line is using data to make something invisible visible.

I also design. Writing for The Chicago Maroon taught me that an argument nobody finishes reading isn’t an argument, and that shaped how I build everything since: the model has to be right, and the result has to land.

Outside the analysis, I’ve led campus initiatives, mentored over a hundred students, and managed teams as project manager and tech lead on multi-quarter research projects.
